# 30DayMaps-2026
Chloe's 30 Day Maps for 2026.

## Day 9: Urban Rural
I'm starting to learn more about electrical design and how we can map electricity. I learned that substations are essentially places where energy is converted into usable electricity to power homes in cities. This way, we can learn to think of America as an electrical grid, outlined by wherever power reaches.
In my map, users can explore where substations reach and where they don't. The white dots are substations in "rural" areas nowhere near a city.
